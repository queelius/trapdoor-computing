\chapter{Proof Locations}
\label{app:proofs}

The book follows one policy for proofs. Results that are elementary and
load-bearing are proved inline, so the argument is self-contained where the
reader meets it. Results whose full proofs are long, or whose home is a
standalone paper under separate submission, are stated with their intuition
and pointed to that paper, to keep the book a treatment rather than a
reproduction. This appendix records where each major result's proof lives, so
that ``proof omitted'' never means ``proof missing'': every result is either
proved in the book or cited to a named companion with the proof.

No result in the book is left without a proof or a pointer to one. The
elementary results, the composition bound, the space duality, the complement
asymmetry, and the orbit bound, are the ones the book proves in full, because
they are short and because the book is their natural home. Everything deeper
belongs to a companion paper and is cited there, which is also why no
separate appendix of deferred proofs is needed: the deferral is to the
papers, mapped above, not to an appendix.
