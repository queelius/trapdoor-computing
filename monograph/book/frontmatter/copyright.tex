This monograph consolidates a family of standalone papers on trapdoor
computing; those papers carry the full proofs of the deeper results
summarized here, and a reconciled formal record governs the shared
definitions. Where the book and a paper differ on a definition, that record,
and this book, follow the corrected form.\\[0.9em]
