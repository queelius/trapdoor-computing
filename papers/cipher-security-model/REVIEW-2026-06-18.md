- **[BLOCKING, logic] (M) clause leakage inequality was backwards.** `I(x;c)/H* <= 1-e` is false: `I(x;c)/H* <= e`, and at `K=1` it forces `e <= 1/2`. **Fixed:** (M) now states the two correct operational bounds, the entropy ratio `e >= 1 - delta - h2(delta)/H*` (Fannes, image-relative) and the single-guess advantage `<= delta/2` (Le Cam), with `1-e = D_KL(Q||U_im)/H*` as the stream's non-uniformity. No input-leakage overclaim.

- **[BLOCKING, citation] Goldreich/ORAM miscited for the leakage-function formalism.** **Fixed:** SSE (Curtmola) uses a leakage function; ORAM (Goldreich) proves access-pattern indistinguishability, now separated, with the leakage-abuse line (`naveed2015`, `cash2015leakage`) added.

- **[m, logic] (A) conditioned on `view_U` not the orbit observation.** **Fixed:** conditions on `V_F(c)`, set form with `|X|` denominator added.
- **[MAJOR, prose] the (delta,R,F) triple was unbound; (C) embedded a theorem in a definition.** **Fixed:** binding sentence added; (C) reframed as a predicate.
